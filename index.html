What it will look for

Built around UK GDPR and PECR.

The privacy mistakes we see most often on property websites, checked automatically.

Trackers before consent

Analytics, ad pixels and cookies that load before a visitor has said yes.

Personal data in requests

Names, emails, phone numbers or postcodes leaking into URLs, headers or third-party payloads.

Cookie banner quality

Is rejecting as easy as accepting? Are choices actually respected after the click?

Session replay & heatmaps

Tools that record keystrokes and form fields on valuation, viewing and mortgage forms.

Transport security

Forms posting over HTTP, mixed content, missing security headers and weak cookie flags.

Transparency signals

A findable privacy notice, a cookie policy that matches reality, and clear controller details.

Why estate agencies

Trust is the whole business.

Every day, agencies collect some of the most personal data there is: who is moving, where, when, and what they can afford.

Yet much of what a website shares happens out of sight, inside scripts the agency never wrote and rarely reviews.

Mercury Ward exists to raise awareness across the industry. It is free, it is open, and it is designed to help, not to name and shame.
